# Performance Goal Plan References

Status: **research evidence for an active PR, not a certification claim**.

## Peer-reviewed evidence

Locke, E. A., & Latham, G. P. (2002). Building a practically useful theory of goal setting and task motivation: A 35-year odyssey. *American Psychologist, 57*(9), 705–717. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705

Cervone, D., & Wood, R. (1995). Goals, feedback, and the differential influence of self-regulatory processes on cognitively complex performance. *Cognitive Therapy and Research, 19*, 519–545. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02230512

## Design interpretation

The evidence supports preserving the exact reviewed goal definition, measurement basis, and feedback context as distinguishable provenance. Orgmetra therefore records digests and a reviewed cadence rather than inferring performance quality from goal existence. The package does not encode automatic difficulty scoring, performance ratings, or employment decisions; those require separate evidence and accountable human review.
