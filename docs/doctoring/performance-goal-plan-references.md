Checked 2026-08-26. NIST still identifies AI RMF 1.0 as the current final framework while a revision is in progress; the Playbook remains a voluntary companion and was updated June 10, 2026. The cited governance material emphasizes clearly differentiated human roles and responsibilities, human oversight, documentation, and accountability. Orgmetra uses those principles as risk-management design evidence rather than claiming NIST certification.

## Design interpretation

The research supports preserving the exact reviewed goal definition, measurement basis, and feedback context as distinguishable provenance. Orgmetra therefore records digests and a reviewed cadence rather than inferring performance quality from goal existence.

The activation lane adds a separate accountable transition from reviewed evidence to operational activation. The exact reviewed human actor and approval instant must be freshly matched to authoritative tenant, Employment, Job, cycle, goal-set, measurement, and cadence evidence. Activation remains explicitly unable to produce a performance rating or employment decision. Any model-assisted content remains untrusted draft evidence and cannot satisfy the human or authoritative activation boundaries by itself.
