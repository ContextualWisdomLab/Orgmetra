## APA 7 reference

International Organization for Standardization. (2025). *ISO 30414:2025 Human resource management — Requirements and recommendations for human capital reporting and disclosure* (2nd ed.). ISO. https://www.iso.org/standard/30414

## Exact public evidence used

The ISO catalogue identifies ISO 30414:2025 as Edition 2, published in August 2025, and lists workforce composition among its core human-capital reporting areas. The prior ISO 30414:2018 edition is withdrawn and the 2025 edition is the current published International Standard.
