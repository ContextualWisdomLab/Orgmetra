# Employment leave review references

Status: active-PR research evidence for ADR 0021. These sources inform a conservative governance boundary; they do not establish certification or universal legal sufficiency.

## APA 7 references

International Organization for Standardization. (2026). *ISO 30201:2026 Human resources management systems—Requirements*. https://www.iso.org/standard/90923.html

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division. (n.d.). *Family and Medical Leave Act employer guide*. Retrieved August 19, 2026, from https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla/employer-guide

## Applied boundary

- ISO 30201:2026 is a published Edition 1 international standard (2026-06) for human-resources management-system requirements. Orgmetra uses it as management-system context for an accountable, controlled worker lifecycle; this PR does not claim ISO certification.
- The U.S. Department of Labor employer guide illustrates a governed leave lifecycle from employee request through restoration to the same or an equivalent job. It is a U.S. FMLA compliance resource, not a universal jurisdictional rule.
- Orgmetra therefore keeps eligibility/medical/family facts in the authoritative leave-case and policy boundaries, while this packet retains only opaque references/digests, business dates, accountable actors, continuity evidence, and a human-review requirement.
- No source here authorizes automated leave eligibility, employment mutation, or downstream benefits/payroll execution.
