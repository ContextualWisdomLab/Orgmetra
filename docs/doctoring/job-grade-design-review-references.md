## Design relevance and limits

The ILO guide describes Job evaluation as assessing different jobs on common and objective criteria and emphasizes a methodology designed to avoid gender bias. That supports Orgmetra's decision to bind a reviewed evaluation-method definition and exact Job-analysis evidence rather than storing an unexplained grade label.

OPM's Factor Evaluation System provides a primary-source example of an auditable factor-based method in which factor levels have explicit point values and total points are mapped through a grade-conversion table. It demonstrates the value of preserving method/version provenance. It does **not** justify copying General Schedule grades into a private enterprise: Orgmetra therefore stores enterprise-local grade/band codes plus a digest of the reviewed architecture and makes no federal-classification claim.

Neither source authorizes an automatic employment or compensation decision. Orgmetra's packet remains human-reviewed evidence awaiting an independent authoritative persistence step and is fixed as `not_authorized_to_assign_grade_or_compensation`.
