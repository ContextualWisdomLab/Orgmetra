# Employment work-capacity review references

Reviewed: 2026-08-24.

## Primary standards sources

International Organization for Standardization. (2021). *ISO/TS 30425:2021 Human resource management—Workforce availability metrics cluster*. https://www.iso.org/standard/77693.html

- ISO currently lists the Technical Specification as published and under systematic review.
- The public abstract distinguishes workforce **working capacity** from existing allocated work, skills, and suitability. Orgmetra uses that scope distinction to keep Employment capacity separate from Assignment allocation; no licensed formula or conformity claim is reproduced.

International Organization for Standardization. (2025). *ISO 30414:2025 Human resource management—Requirements and recommendations for human capital reporting and disclosure* (2nd ed.). https://www.iso.org/standard/86106.html

- ISO lists the 2025 edition as published and identifies workforce composition among the standard's human-capital reporting areas.
- Orgmetra uses this only to support historically reconstructable, auditable workforce evidence. This package does not claim ISO 30414 certification or conformity.
