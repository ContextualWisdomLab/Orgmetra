## Applied boundary

- ISO 30434:2023 is used for the high-level workforce-allocation framing: allocation decisions should be managed as a process that accounts for stakeholders, including workers, and supports documentation and communication. Orgmetra does not reproduce proprietary clauses or annex content.
- ISO 30435:2023 supports treating authoritative scope snapshots and reviewed evidence provenance as data-quality inputs rather than assuming that syntactically valid identifiers prove correct workforce relationships.
- ISO 30201:2026 is a current published HR management-system requirements standard. The package uses its public abstract only as support for integrating HR process controls with organizational management and risk management; no conformity claim is made.
- The OPM GPPA is a U.S. federal, jurisdiction-specific operational example. Its current public site identifies Chapter 14 for promotions, reassignments and position changes and states that the GPPA is continuously updated. Orgmetra does not generalize federal procedural rules into a universal employment-law rule.
