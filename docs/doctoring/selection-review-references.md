# Selection review references

These references support the active-PR human selection-review packet boundary. They do not claim ISO certification or replace jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## APA 7 references

## Design implications recorded here

- Recruitment and selection remain governed organizational processes rather than an isolated model-scoring step.
- Selection evidence must remain job related and support an accountable employment decision boundary.
- AI-based assessment evidence receives the same validity, fairness, and job-relatedness scrutiny as other selection evidence; model output is not converted into an authoritative decision by this package.
- The packet therefore carries opaque provenance and evidence identifiers, requires a human decision, and intentionally excludes candidate PII and free-form model output.
