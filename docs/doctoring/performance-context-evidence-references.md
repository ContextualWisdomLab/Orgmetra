# Performance context evidence: doctoring references

## Evidence status

Reviewed 2026-08-23. These references support preserving work-context provenance when interpreting performance criteria. They do not authorize automatic causal adjustment, performance ratings, or employment decisions.

## Authoritative professional guidance

Society for Industrial and Organizational Psychology. (2018). *Principles for the validation and use of personnel selection procedures* (5th ed.). *Industrial and Organizational Psychology, 11*(S1), 1–97. https://doi.org/10.1017/iop.2018.195

- The fifth edition was approved by the APA Council of Representatives in August 2018 as an authoritative guidelines document for employee selection testing.
- Design implication: validation evidence should preserve the work/criterion scope needed to support the intended inference rather than treat observed outcomes as context-free.

Society for Industrial and Organizational Psychology. (2023, January). *Considerations and recommendations for the validation and use of AI-based assessments for employee selection.*

- The statement advises understanding variation in the work and identifying irrelevant sources of variance when defining validation outcomes.
- Design implication: keep reviewed context provenance available to accountable analysts while keeping model output and employment-decision authority outside this packet.

## Peer-reviewed empirical evidence

O'Connor, E. J., Peters, L. H., Pooyan, A., Weekley, J., Frank, B., & Erenkrantz, B. (1984). Situational constraint effects on performance, affective reactions, and turnover: A field replication and extension. *Journal of Applied Psychology, 69*(4), 663–672. https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.69.4.663

- Field data were collected from managers and supervisors in one company; higher situational constraints were associated with poorer performance ratings and other outcomes.
- Design implication: opportunity/constraint evidence can be material to criterion interpretation, but this association does not identify a person-specific causal correction.

Steel, R. P., & Mento, A. J. (1986). Impact of situational constraints on subjective and objective criteria of managerial job performance. *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 37*(2), 254–265. https://doi.org/10.1016/0749-5978(86)90054-3

- The field study examined 438 branch managers and reported differences across high- and low-constraint settings for supervisory ratings, self-ratings, and an objective performance measure.
- Design implication: provenance should distinguish contextual evidence from the criterion itself so later validation can model or stratify context transparently instead of silently rewriting the individual outcome.

## Peer-reviewed synthesis used for construct framing

Campbell, J. P., & Wiernik, B. M. (2015). The modeling and assessment of work performance. *Annual Review of Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior, 2*, 47–74. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-032414-111427

- This review summarizes performance content, dynamics, and assessment issues.
- Design implication: the packet is context provenance around a criterion; it is not itself a definition of performance or a replacement for construct-valid criterion design.

## Engineering interpretation

The implemented contract is deliberately narrower than the research questions: it records bounded source references, business-time scope, and digests. Statistical claims require a separate governed analysis with explicit sampling, multilevel/multiple-membership/temporal structure where scientifically relevant, uncertainty, diagnostics, and human review.
