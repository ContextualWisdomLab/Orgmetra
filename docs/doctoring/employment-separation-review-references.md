# Employment separation review references

Status: active-PR research evidence for ADR 0020. These sources inform a conservative governance boundary; they do not establish certification or universal legal sufficiency.

## APA 7 references

International Organization for Standardization. (2026). *ISO 30201:2026 Human resources management systems—Requirements*. https://www.iso.org/standard/90923.html

National Institute of Standards and Technology. (2020). *Security and privacy controls for information systems and organizations* (NIST Special Publication 800-53, Revision 5). U.S. Department of Commerce. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/final

U.S. Office of Personnel Management. (2026, July). *The guide to processing personnel actions*. https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/data-analysis-documentation/personnel-documentation/processing-personnel-actions/guide_to_processing_personnel_actions.pdf

## Applied boundary

- ISO 30201:2026 is used as current HR-management-system requirements context for a controlled, accountable employment lifecycle process.
- OPM's July 2026 Guide contains a dedicated Chapter 31 for separations other than retirement and illustrates that separation processing is a governed personnel-action process. It is a U.S. federal example, not a universal jurisdictional rule for Orgmetra customers.
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 Personnel Security, including PS-4 Personnel Termination, informs the need to coordinate employment termination with access removal and organizational-property handling. Orgmetra binds a reviewed access-deprovisioning plan but leaves identity/provider execution to the published owner boundary.
- None of these references authorizes automated separation decisions. Orgmetra keeps scope resolution, approval, HRIS mutation, and downstream execution explicitly separate and human-accountable.
