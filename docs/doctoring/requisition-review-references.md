## Design implications recorded here

- Recruiting preparation should identify accountable stakeholders and retain evidence that the opening is grounded in organizational need rather than a free-form request.
- Job requirements used in later selection should be traceable to current job-analysis evidence and should distinguish entry requirements from competencies expected to be learned after hire.
- A review packet should bind Job requirements and authorization evidence without becoming a second Job Analysis or finance system of record.
- An exact Position is one seat; multi-opening requisitions therefore remain Job-scoped until seats are individually allocated.
