## Protected-main boundary

Protected `develop` remains authoritative for integrated HRIS behavior. This active PR adds only a pre-mutation review evidence contract. Its process-local issuance registry is defense in depth for the live Python object, not durable identity, cryptographic attestation, authorization, or persisted immutability. Durable systems must persist the already-verified canonical evidence and digest through Orgmetra's immutable audit/outbox boundary. This PR does not claim unmerged People mutation, payroll/benefits, identity, calendar, or other downstream execution capability.
