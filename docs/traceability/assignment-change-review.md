The packet deliberately does not prove actor identity, Position capacity, policy/legal permissibility, worker consultation, collective-agreement requirements, or mutation success. Those remain authoritative runtime/policy evidence and must not be inferred from the packet digest or reference-string inequality. The process-local issuance registry is deliberately not a distributed attestation store: durable uniqueness, authorization, retention, and immutable audit/outbox remain responsibilities of authoritative Orgmetra host/persistence boundaries.
