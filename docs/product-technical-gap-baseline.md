# Product and technical gap baseline

Verified: 2026-09-16 (Asia/Seoul) for Orgmetra protected/product refs. External owner-repository evidence is treated as dependency context and must be re-fetched in its canonical owner lane before mutation or release claims.

This is Orgmetra’s durable commercialization baseline, not merge authorization and not a frozen PR inventory. Volatile PR heads, workflow-run IDs, queue states, reviews, mergeability and base tips are live GitHub truth and must be fetched again before every material action.

## 1. Product thesis and buyer outcome

Orgmetra is the ContextualWisdomLab HRIS/HCM system of record for authoritative People, Organization, Position, Assignment, Job Architecture, FJA/KSAO, Talent, Assessment and Workforce truth. Its buyer value is evidence-preserving decision infrastructure: what employment fact was true, when it was true, what evidence justified a high-impact decision, who acted, what purpose authorized access, and how later outcomes validate the original job/selection model.

Primary users are HR operations owners, HRIS administrators, recruiters, hiring managers, job-analysis specialists, psychometricians/people-analytics scientists, compliance/audit reviewers, workers and enterprise integration engineers.

The buyer lifecycle is Job requirements/evidence → Candidate evidence → accountable human selection/offer → authoritative worker/employment/assignment truth → job-relevant performance evidence → validation/fairness evidence → purpose-bound operational workflows.

Post-hire Talent Management is not yet protected architecture. Active Proposed ADR 0292 selects a dedicated `talent_management` bounded context as the current design direction for talent pools, succession, internal mobility and employee-controlled career interests/preferences. Until that ADR is accepted and normally integrated, `people_core`, `organization_core`, `job_architecture`, `performance_management` and `workforce_validation` retain their existing protected truth; the proposed Talent owner may reference released/versioned evidence but may not copy or mutate those owners' Person, Employment, Assignment, Position, Job/KSAO, performance, assessment or validity/fairness truth. The Proposed owner contract preserves purpose-minimized immutable decision-opportunity/stage-transition provenance for each material Talent stage so later scientific analysis has an exact denominator. That evidence includes exact entry-level opportunity membership, a semantic opportunity occurrence identity that is idempotent across retry/replay yet distinct across legitimate repeated opportunities for the same worker, and an explicit versioned counting/analysis unit. Append-only correction history resolves each closed semantic occurrence to exactly one canonical active terminal entry; the mutually exclusive canonical terminal set explicitly includes `advanced`, `not_evaluable`, `policy_inapplicable`, `human_excluded`, `automated_excluded_or_suppressed`, `withdrawn`, `unavailable`, and `failed`, with `unavailable` reserved for unresolved evidence/authorization/owner availability at closure and `failed` for unresolved error/failure. Superseded history and processing/retry attempts remain auditable but are not denominator or terminal rows, and `not_evaluable` cannot silently absorb unavailable or failed cases. #399 adds the separate manifest-level authority needed when a closed opportunity manifest is later replaced or reconsidered: same-lineage corrections keep a stable manifest lineage, immutable version/digest and deterministic acyclic supersession; consumers choose an explicit historical/as-recorded or corrected-as-of view at a stated cutoff rather than an implicit `latest` or predecessor+replacement union. The manifest digest is itself reproducible evidence under a versioned canonical semantic field-set/projection, deterministic serialization/ordering/normalization, explicit digest algorithm/domain separator/version and metadata-contract version, with consumer recomputation/validation. #400 adds a separate policy-semantics boundary: same-lineage `corrected_as_of` traversal is allowed only inside an explicitly evidenced compatible policy regime, while material changes to eligibility meaning, threshold/cut score, ranking/order, required-evidence meaning, suppression/routing or equivalent entry/advancement/exclusion rules start a new regime and cannot masquerade as record correction. Semantic-preserving editorial/no-op reseals may remain in the same regime only with explicit equivalence evidence. #401 makes that regime identity itself reproducible and dependency-closed: Talent derives or verifies it from a versioned semantic projection with explicit canonical-representation and digest algorithm/domain/version metadata plus the immutable released identity/version/digest of every material external dependency; caller-supplied IDs, mutable/floating refs, live cross-service state, serializer accidents and presentation-only metadata are not authority. #402 separates those external artifacts' exact integrity/version evidence from their downstream selection-semantic authority: Talent consumes an owner-issued released semantic-dependency receipt or equivalent ACL projection and never reconstructs foreign owner truth; one digest serves both roles only when the upstream contract explicitly gives it that semantic meaning and stability/equivalence rules. #403 then binds the intended policy projection and owner receipts to the procedure actually executed: the trusted Talent boundary records the final immutable dependencies/receipts and material runtime parameters resolved for the canonical terminal stage attempt, compares expected versus observed deterministically, and makes mismatched, stale-cache, fallback, alias-only, missing/extra or unverifiable execution non-authorizing. Released scientific results bind the exact selected manifest lineage/version/digest/digest-contract/view/as-of, governing policy-regime identity, semantic-projection schema/version, external owner semantic receipts, material dependency provenance and actual execution-congruence evidence so later corrections or materially changed/executed procedures create new evidence versions instead of rewriting prior results. Protected demographic attributes, scientific grouping/estimand choice and fairness/validity verdicts remain outside Talent authority and are joined/analyzed only through released purpose-authorized `workforce_validation` contracts.

## 5. Effective GitHub governance

Orgmetra’s effective default-branch control plane is inherited organization ruleset **18156473 — `CWL Central required workflows`**, re-verified on 2026-09-16. Current live parameters are:

- one approving review required;
- stale reviews dismissed after push;
- review-thread resolution required;
- extra approval required for unattributed changes;
- merge and squash are the allowed merge methods;
- deletion and non-fast-forward protection are enabled;
- required workflows are `opencode-review`, `pr-review-merge-scheduler`, `security-scan`, `strix`, `sast-semgrep`, `noema-review`, and `codeql-pr`;
- `OrganizationAdmin/always` bypass is exposed.

Routine bypass, self-approval, synthetic reviewer identity, gate weakening or treating model/bot review as the required human approval are not acceptable repair strategies. Central ruleset mutation remains owned by `ContextualWisdomLab/.github`; Orgmetra records the dependency and canary evidence but does not create a leaf workflow shim or copy central policy source.

## 8. Data, scientific and AI invariants

- DDD subdomains, bounded contexts, UL, aggregates, entities, value objects, domain services, repositories, events and invariants must agree across code/API/DB/tests.
- Relational authoritative truth remains normalized; read models are separated explicitly when measured need justifies them.
- Tenant, bitemporal, lock/partition and item-level UPSERT/idempotency contracts are tested at database boundaries.
- Tenant authority is bound before acquiring advisory locks, leases or other database-global coordination state; eventual RLS rejection is not a substitute for the owning domain check, including for privileged maintenance and test principals.
- Idempotent replay binds every server-owned result field, including the original database timestamp, and canonicalizes session-sensitive temporal input inside the owner boundary. A uniqueness violation or presentation-equivalent timestamp is not replay evidence.
- Storage-backed value objects and receipts that make runtime type part of equality must keep every relied-on rich comparison and hash operation coherent with the same exact-type contract. A built-in container superclass must not make `==` and `!=` disagree; acceptance covers same-type equality/hash consistency, sibling-result inequality, and ordinary-container inequality in both operand directions.
- Database/security acceptance runs temporary principals with collision-resistant per-execution identities. Failure cleanup remains best-effort so it cannot mask the causal assertion, while normal completion fails closed unless cleanup is verified.
- Acceptance executables consume an immutable exact-candidate tree covering transitive migrations, helpers and configuration, not only hashed root scripts. They start from an empty or explicit allowlisted environment with a reviewed executable path and principal-owned disposable HOME, TMPDIR and XDG directories; ambient CI variables and mutable-worktree paths are not execution authority.
- Commercial buyer-path evidence binds the canonical route and owner representation, not merely a successful-looking body: request semantics, redirect/proxy policy, authenticated HTTPS with certificate verification, purpose-bound cache/privacy headers, response media/envelope semantics, iteration/outcome/latency cardinality and cleanup evidence must all fail closed when the measured path is ambiguous or differs from the owner contract.
- Evidence-byte/provenance attestation and deployed-candidate identity are separate trust boundaries. Signing or sealing caller-authored `candidate_sha` / `observed_service_sha` fields does not prove which service was measured. Positive commercial SLO acceptance requires an independently authenticated source SHA → immutable artifact/image digest → deployment/revision → measured HTTPS origin and measurement-window chain, plus production-equivalent PostgreSQL schema/index/RLS, authorization and runtime evidence; both owner contracts must be released/versioned before a positive receipt exists.
- Multiple assignments/memberships and time-varying context remain representable; person-level inference must not silently commit atomistic fallacy.
- Per-person decision provenance is not a scientific denominator. Every material Talent stage that can change eligibility, inclusion/exclusion, shortlist/slate membership, ranking, suppression, thresholding or routing must bind an immutable opportunity-set/stage-transition manifest to the exact tenant, purpose, process/version, stage/order, criteria/policy/evidence versions and effective/system-time or measurement window. The evidence preserves explicit `not_evaluable`, stale/missing-evidence, authorization/owner-unavailable, policy-inapplicable, human-excluded, automated-excluded-or-suppressed, withdrawn and failure/error states plus relevant multiple-membership, cross-classified and temporal structure; downstream survivors cannot substitute for the original opportunity set.
- A Talent opportunity manifest is not scientifically complete with only aggregate counts or a digest. Each closed stage preserves exact entry-level membership, a versioned counting/analysis unit, and one semantic opportunity occurrence identity per denominator entry. Retry/replay of the same semantic opportunity is idempotent and cannot create extra denominator rows; distinct legitimate opportunities involving the same worker remain distinct. Processing attempts are many-to-one operational telemetry. Append-only corrections preserve the same semantic occurrence identity unless a genuinely new opportunity occurred and resolve through a deterministic acyclic supersession chain to exactly one canonical active terminal entry. Superseded historical rows and processing/retry attempts remain immutable evidence but are excluded from denominator and terminal totals. The canonical closed-stage terminal set includes `advanced`, `not_evaluable`, `policy_inapplicable`, `human_excluded`, `automated_excluded_or_suppressed`, `withdrawn`, `unavailable`, and `failed`: unresolved stale/missing evidence or authorization/owner availability resolves to `unavailable`; unresolved error/failure resolves to `failed`; `not_evaluable` remains a distinct explicit evaluability outcome rather than a catch-all. Closure must reconcile `entry_count == count(unique semantic opportunity occurrence identities) == count(canonical active terminal entries) == sum(counts of every canonical active terminal classification)`. Zero or multiple active terminal entries for one occurrence, cyclic/dangling/ambiguous correction chains, remapping or omitting terminal classes, or counting superseded/attempt rows are acceptance failures. `talent_management` owns only this purpose-minimized process provenance; `workforce_validation` explicitly chooses and records occurrence/case/person grouping and the scientific estimand from released evidence. Protected-group attributes and fairness/validity/scientific verdicts remain outside Talent and must not be reconstructed by cross-service SQL or copied demographics.
- Immutable Talent manifest replacements have a second, manifest-level authority contract. Same-stage/cohort corrections retain one stable opaque manifest lineage; every closed version is immutable and digest-addressable with an explicit supersession predecessor; the lineage is deterministic and acyclic. Manifest digest identity is independently reproducible from a versioned canonical semantic field-set/projection, deterministic serialization/ordering/normalization rules, explicit digest algorithm/domain separator/version and metadata-contract version, and consumers recompute/validate it rather than accepting an opaque checksum. Equivalent manifest semantics under the same contract must not receive different identities because of serializer ordering, whitespace or presentation-only metadata. A consumer selects exactly one version for an explicit historical/as-recorded or corrected-as-of view and declared cutoff. Superseded manifests stay audit evidence but are never blindly unioned with replacements; an unqualified `latest` is not authority. Cross-version opportunity additions/removals or terminal changes require semantic-occurrence-level controlled delta provenance. Every released `workforce_validation` or buyer/scientific result records the exact selected manifest lineage/version/digest/digest-contract/view/as-of, so a later correction produces a new result/evidence version instead of retroactively changing the old denominator.
- Talent manifest correction lineage does not establish selection-policy compatibility. Every material stage and closed manifest additionally binds the exact `decision_policy_version` and canonical material policy-regime/semantic identity or digest. Same-lineage `corrected_as_of` traversal is permitted only when deterministic owner evidence proves the material selection semantics compatible. A material change to eligibility meaning, threshold/cut score, ranking/order method, required-evidence meaning, suppression/routing or another rule that changes entry, evaluability, advancement, exclusion or reachability of human review starts a new policy regime; it cannot be hidden as an occurrence/manifest correction. A semantic-preserving editorial/no-op reseal may remain in the same regime only with explicit semantic-equivalence evidence. Historical as-recorded results remain bound to the procedure actually used, and re-analysis under a materially changed regime creates new simulation/counterfactual/analysis evidence. Released downstream results bind both exact manifest authority and governing policy-regime identity. `workforce_validation` retains validity, fairness/adverse-impact, estimand, transportability and interpretation authority; Talent owns only purpose-minimized process/procedure provenance.
- A Talent policy-regime identity is owner-derived reproducible evidence, not a caller assertion or opaque hash. Its versioned semantic projection explicitly includes every material local policy field and every released external dependency capable of changing entry, evaluability, advancement, exclusion, ranking/order, thresholding, suppression, routing or human-review reachability, while explicitly excluding presentation/editorial metadata that cannot change those semantics. The projection binds schema/version, canonical-representation identifier/version, digest algorithm/domain/version and each material dependency’s released owner identity plus immutable version/digest; mutable branches, floating labels, unversioned external names and live cross-service table state are invalid inputs. Talent recomputes or verifies the identity rather than trusting a supplied `policy_regime_id`/digest. Same-regime equivalence is deterministic equality or separately versioned/auditable equivalence-migration evidence with immutable source/target projections, rule version, outcome and provenance. Every released manifest and downstream result retains enough projection/dependency provenance for independent reproduction. Collision or digest mismatch fails closed for explicit integrity resolution and is not a semantic-equivalence verdict.
- External artifact integrity and downstream selection-semantic authority are separate bounded-context evidence contracts. For each material external dependency, Talent consumes a released owner semantic receipt or equivalent ACL projection that binds owner/context/contract/version, exact artifact integrity, downstream-material semantic identity/projection, applicability scope and owner-issued deterministic equivalence evidence where required. One digest may intentionally serve both integrity and semantic identity only when the upstream released contract defines that meaning. Generic artifact hashes, version labels or Talent-side parsing of foreign Job/FJA/KSAO/assessment/performance/model/tool internals cannot establish semantic compatibility; missing, conflicting, stale, wrong-purpose, wrong-tenant or ambiguous owner authority fails closed.
- Intended policy provenance and actual stage execution provenance are separate trust boundaries. Every material stage's canonical terminal attempt binds the governing policy projection/owner receipts to trusted observed-resolved immutable dependency identities/versions/digests and material runtime parameters, then records a versioned deterministic `execution_congruent`, `execution_incongruent`, or controlled unavailable/not-verifiable result. Requested aliases, provider/model groups, cache keys and caller-submitted resolved metadata are not final execution authority. Missing/extra dependencies, fallback, stale cache, immutable-identity mismatch or retry-to-retry dependency drift make the material effect non-authorizing until resolved. Released manifests and downstream scientific results retain this execution evidence so the studied procedure can be distinguished from the procedure merely declared.
- Material mathematical/psychometric/EDA/vector/linear/matrix/token-size computation is Rust-first with bounded CPU parallelism and justified GPU parity.
- Psychometric acceptance uses true-parameter recovery, RMSE, bias, coverage and reproducibility. Synthetic data is unit-test evidence, not real-world acceptance.
- LLM output is draft/supporting evidence only. LLM work consumes released contextual-orchestrator API/client/schema contracts; GitHub Actions request only `orchestrator/free` through the gateway token and do not hard-code provider/model/group/paid fallback policy. Capability absence fails closed and is repaired in the canonical orchestrator owner.
- Under Proposed ADR 0292, post-hire Talent planning consumes released owner evidence rather than duplicating Person/Employment/Assignment/Position/Job/KSAO/performance/assessment/validation truth. This remains proposed until normally integrated.
- No universal `potential`, `readiness`, `fit`, `talent_score` or “high-potential” scalar becomes HRIS truth without an explicit construct, intended use, versioned evidence, uncertainty, validity/generalizability limits and fairness/adverse-impact analysis.
- Under the current Proposed Talent contract, `fully_automated_decision` is non-authorizing provenance for imported/historical/external high-impact outcomes; Orgmetra does not use that mode to finalize a high-impact Talent decision. `ai_assisted_human_decision` requires substantive human-intervention evidence plus accountable-human confirmation. Jurisdiction-specific applicability and rights remain versioned legal/compliance-policy determinations rather than implications of the mode label alone.

## 9. Security, privacy and compliance posture

Orgmetra targets evidence readiness for CSAP/SOC 2-style enterprise review without claiming certification. PII controls are purpose-bound and operationally usable: tenant/actor/purpose/resource/lifetime authorization, least privilege, forced RLS where applicable, encryption, immutable audit evidence, retention/export/delete lifecycle, incident/recovery evidence and separately governed break-glass operation.

Customer-facing language describes the user’s next action and evidence state, not internal repository/schema/model/agent boundaries.

## 11. Release gate

A commercial release is allowed only from one freshly fetched protected `develop` head that simultaneously proves:

- release-scope dependencies integrated in causal order;
- no unresolved valid review finding;
- every required exact-head deterministic gate terminal-success, including authoritative Dependency Review rather than substitutes;
- satisfiable protected-branch admission without synthetic approval or routine admin bypass;
- buyer vertical-slice E2E, accessibility, security, recovery and load evidence terminal-success;
- migrations/rollback/backup-restore and tenant/purpose/audit invariants against production-equivalent PostgreSQL;
- documentation/ADR/TRD/API/events/schema/UI copy matching released implementation;
- release version and CHANGELOG bound to the exact protected commit and migration/API compatibility;
- immutable package/release plus SBOM/provenance/reproducibility/rollback evidence; and
- no temporary purpose-complete self-modifying/source-fix workflow.

Until this evidence exists, version `0.1.0` and zero published releases are correctly treated as pre-commercial integration state rather than a release-management defect to paper over.
